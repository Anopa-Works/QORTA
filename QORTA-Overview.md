# QORTA — How It Works
**Digital Ordering Platform for Restaurants**

---

## The Problem It Solves

Most restaurants still rely on handwritten tickets, shouting across the floor, or outdated POS systems that don't connect staff in real time. Orders get missed, communication breaks down, and customers are left waiting with no idea what's happening.

QORTA fixes that.

---

## The Full Flow

### 1. Customer Orders
A customer scans a QR code at their table and browses the restaurant's menu on their phone — no app download needed. They add items to their cart and place their order in seconds.

### 2. Kitchen Receives Instantly
The moment an order is placed, it appears on the kitchen screen in real time. No paper tickets, no relay through staff. The kitchen sees exactly what to prepare.

### 3. Kitchen Marks Ready
When the food is ready, the kitchen taps one button — **"Mark Ready."** That's the only action they need to take.

### 4. Waiter is Notified Instantly
The waiter assigned to that table gets an immediate notification on their screen. They collect the order and deliver it — no guessing, no checking, no shouting.

---

## Two Modes, One System

### Self-Ordering Mode
Designed for fast-casual and takeaway restaurants where customers handle their own ordering. A QR code is placed on each table or at the counter. The customer scans it, the menu loads instantly on their phone, and they browse, select items, and place their order — all without speaking to anyone. No app, no account, no friction. The order goes directly to the kitchen the moment it's placed. This mode keeps staff focused on food and service rather than taking orders manually.

### Service Mode
Designed for sit-down restaurants where a waiter attends to each table. When this mode is active, the customer-facing menu is disabled — customers cannot self-order. Instead, the waiter logs into their own dashboard, selects the customer's table, browses the full menu, and places the order on their behalf. The kitchen receives it instantly. When the food is ready, the waiter gets a notification directly on their screen telling them which table's order is ready for collection. The cashier handles payment separately. This keeps the kitchen focused on cooking and the waiter focused on service — with no paper, no shouting, and no missed orders.

The restaurant switches between these two modes from the admin panel in seconds — no technical knowledge needed.

## Who Uses What

**Customer** → Scans QR code, browses menu on phone, places order, tracks order status live

**Waiter** → Logs in, selects table, places order on customer's behalf, gets notified when order is ready

**Kitchen** → Sees all incoming orders on a live screen, marks orders ready with one tap

**Admin** → Manages the menu, views full order history, switches between service and self-ordering mode
