About Us

Powering Zimbabwe with Cleaner Energy

We specialize in manufacturing premium charcoal briquettes and firelighters that provide affordable, reliable heat while promoting sustainable energy use.

Country Flames was born from the vision of a cleaner, more sustainable Zimbabwe. Our briquettes are crafted from 100% recycled biomass materials, turning agricultural waste into high-performance fuel.

From households to restaurants, our products serve communities across Harare and beyond with energy solutions kind to both your wallet and the environment.
